03

Regulatory radar

What is landing on regulated businesses in Europe, and when. Updated as the timetable moves.

  1. Dec 2024

    MiCA fully applicable

    Crypto-asset service providers across the EU now operate under a single licensing and conduct regime. Transitional periods vary by member state.

    In force
  2. Jan 2025

    DORA applies

    Financial entities must run ICT-risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing and third-party oversight. 2026 is the year supervisors look for evidence.

    In force
  3. 2026

    Bank of Lithuania inspection focus

    Roughly twenty planned and fifteen unplanned inspections targeting AML/CTF controls, fraud prevention and payment-service conduct. Licence revocations show enforcement is real.

    Now
  4. Jul 2027

    AMLR single rulebook

    The EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation replaces national transpositions with directly applicable rules on CDD, beneficial ownership and group-wide policies.

    Upcoming
  5. 2028

    AMLA direct supervision

    The new EU authority begins directly supervising selected high-risk cross-border entities and coordinating national supervisors.

    Upcoming
